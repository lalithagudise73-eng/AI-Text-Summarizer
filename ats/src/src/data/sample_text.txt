Artificial intelligence is changing many areas of modern technology.
Machine learning allows computers to learn patterns from data.
Natural language processing helps computers understand human language.
AI systems are used in education, healthcare, transportation, and finance.
Text summarization is an important natural language processing application.
It helps users understand long documents in less time.
Extractive summarization selects important sentences from the original document.
TF-IDF can be used to measure the importance of words in different sentences.
The most important sentences can then be combined to create a shorter summary.
